MOTOR VEHICLE INSURANCE POLICY
Policy ID: CG-MOTOR-2026-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Expiry Date: 2026-12-31

============================================================
1. POLICY COVERAGE
============================================================

This policy provides coverage for accidental physical damage to the
insured vehicle caused by:

- Collision with another vehicle
- Collision with a fixed or stationary object
- Accidental overturning
- Fire caused by an accidental event
- Theft of the insured vehicle
- Natural events including flood, storm, cyclone and earthquake

Coverage applies only to the vehicle identified in the policy schedule.

============================================================
2. INSURED VALUE
============================================================

The Insured Declared Value (IDV) of the vehicle is:

INR 500,000

The maximum payable amount for a covered claim cannot exceed the
applicable IDV.

============================================================
3. CLAIM REPORTING WINDOW
============================================================

The policyholder must report an accident or damage claim within
7 calendar days from the date of the incident.

Theft claims must be reported to the insurer and the police as soon
as reasonably possible.

Claims reported outside the applicable reporting period require
manual review and may be declined if the delay is not reasonably
explained.

============================================================
4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
============================================================

For an ACCIDENT claim, the following documents are required:

- Claim Form
- Repair Estimate
- Incident Description

For a THEFT claim, the following documents are required:

- Claim Form
- FIR
- Incident Description

For a NATURAL_EVENT claim, the following documents are required:

- Claim Form
- Repair Estimate
- Incident Description

Additional documents may be requested when required to establish
the circumstances or amount of the claim.

============================================================
5. EXCLUSIONS
============================================================

The following are excluded from coverage:

- Normal wear and tear
- Mechanical breakdown without an insured accidental event
- Electrical or mechanical failure caused solely by internal defect
- Damage caused intentionally by the policyholder
- Damage occurring while the vehicle is being used for an
unauthorized purpose
- Damage caused while driving under the influence of alcohol or
  prohibited substances

An excluded cause cannot be treated as a covered accidental loss
without additional evidence establishing a covered event.

============================================================
6. CLAIM AMOUNT
============================================================

A claim amount must be supported by reasonable evidence such as a
repair estimate, invoice, or other relevant documentation.

The insurer should not approve an amount above the applicable IDV.

Where the claimed amount cannot be reasonably established from the
available evidence, the claim must be sent for manual review.

============================================================
7. CONTRADICTORY INFORMATION
============================================================

If material information differs between the claim form, incident
description, FIR, repair estimate, or other supporting documents,
the contradiction must be surfaced.

The system must not silently select one version of contradictory
information.

Material contradictions require manual review.

============================================================
8. UNCERTAINTY AND MANUAL REVIEW
============================================================

A claim must be escalated for manual review when:

- Material evidence is contradictory
- Required evidence is unavailable and the claim cannot be assessed
- The cause of damage is unclear
- The available evidence does not establish whether the loss is
  covered
- The claimed amount cannot be reasonably established
- A potentially high-risk or unusual situation is identified

The assistant must explain why the claim was escalated.

============================================================
9. DECISION GUIDANCE
============================================================

APPROVE:

Use only when the available evidence supports coverage, required
information is present, no material contradiction exists, and the
claim amount is within the applicable IDV.

REJECT:

Use when available evidence clearly establishes that the claim is
excluded or otherwise clearly outside the policy.

REQUEST_INFORMATION:

Use when the claim may be covered but required information or
documents are missing.

ESCALATE:

Use when the claim contains material contradictions, significant
uncertainty, or circumstances requiring human judgement.

============================================================
10. ASSISTANT LIMITATIONS
============================================================

This assistant is an evidence review system.

It must not invent documents, events, policy clauses, dates, amounts,
or facts.

Every important finding must identify its supporting evidence.

Policy findings must reference the relevant policy section.

The assistant does not determine that fraud has occurred.

Suspicious, contradictory, or unusual information should be surfaced
for human investigation rather than treated as proof of fraud.
